Water Quality Monitoring (WQM)

A web-based system for reporting and managing water-quality complaints.
Citizens can submit issues like leakage or contamination, and administrators can track, update, and resolve them through a unified dashboard.

Problem Overview

City administrations struggle with scattered and slow complaint-handling processes.
This platform centralizes submissions and delivers a clear workflow from reporting to resolution.

Core Objectives

Provide a simple interface for citizens to raise water-related complaints.

Give administrators a dashboard to view, update, and resolve issues.

Offer data visualization for better decision-making and faster response.

SDG 6: Clean Water & Sanitation

The system directly supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 6 by enabling faster detection and handling of water issues.

Goal: Ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all.
Target 6.1: Universal access to safe and affordable drinking water.
Indicator 6.1.1: Population using safely-managed drinking-water services.

System Thinking

The project uses system-thinking techniques to understand water-quality dynamics:

Causal Maps – identify relationships and root causes

Behaviour Over Time (BoT) – track complaint trends

Feedback Loops – highlight reinforcing and balancing patterns
